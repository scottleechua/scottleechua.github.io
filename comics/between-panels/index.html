Between Panels

MĀNOA: A Pacific Journal of International Writing

buy the digital issue
The first two pages.

Between Panels is a short comic about making comics in, on, and through history — in collaboration with the inimitable Renren Galeno.

It was published in the Philippine spotlight issue of the Mānoa Journal (guest edited by Laurel Flores Fantauzzo) and graciously nominated for the 2025 Pushcart Prize.

Between Panels was part of the 2026 Nib & Ink Festival Marketplace, a digital comics fair organized by Cartoonists United.
