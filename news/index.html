An archive of all previous announcements.

Jan 2025 Between Panels, my comic with Renren Galeno about making comics in, on, and through history, was published in MĀNOA: A Pacific Journal of International Writing!
Nov 2024 I talked about bringing Actually Useful AI Systems: From Experiment to Production for the Ateneo de Manila University Mathematics Research Seminar Series.
Feb 2024 I published Spam of the Times, a micro-essay about spam and digital detritus with interactive graphs of my own text message history.
Aug 2023 I had a great discussion with Ethan Chua, Lawrence Ypil, and Sydney Paige Guerrero in a roundtable on Philippine speculative fiction for the Bulosan Center Affiliates Talk Series!
Apr 2023 My paper on Philippine science fiction with Ethan Chua has been published in Philippine Studies: Historical and Ethnographic Viewpoints.
Nov 2022 I presented a study with Guillem Riambau and Tim Wertz on grades and learning outcomes at the 60th Annual Meeting and Conference of the Philippine Economic Society.
Jul 2022 I ran a workshop on reading and writing for a wonderful bunch of primary school students at the Scholastic Readers Camp!
Apr 2022 My paper on lying behavior with Jessica Chang and Guillem Riambau has been published in the Journal of Economic Psychology.

Jun 2021 I presented my paper on trust and darknet markets at WEIS 2021.
Feb 2021 I ran a workshop on sequential storytelling at the Yale-NUS College Writers’ Centre Comics and Zines Fest!
Dec 2019 My English translation of Enrique Villasis’ poetry collection Buwaya (Crocodile) has received the Gabo Prize for literature in translation.
